Reproduce with scripts/convert_open_dictionaries.py, followed by
scripts/build_dictionary_catalog.py. Preserve notices and applicable licenses
when distributing these datasets or modifications.

skey_wubi86_high.tsv, skey_pinyin_high.tsv and skey_english_high.tsv are initially
empty, locally editable priority sources. All six files can be replaced by a
validated same-name import and recompiled. Successful compilation replaces the
runtime snapshot; no previous dictionary is used as a fallback.
